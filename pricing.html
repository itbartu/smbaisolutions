Frequently asked questions

Things worth knowing.

Why do some domain names cost more?

Domain extensions have different registry and registrar pricing. Some individual names are designated as premium, and first-year promotions may differ from renewals. A more expensive domain is not automatically better for search rankings.

Do I own my domain?

We recommend registering the domain in your name or your company's name, with clear access and transfer arrangements. Ownership and account responsibilities are confirmed before purchase.

Is the domain included in the monthly plan?

We can include an allocation for standard domain renewals in a recurring quote. Premium registration fees or unusual renewal prices may be billed separately and will be disclosed in advance.

Can I use a logo I already own?

Absolutely. With your permission, your existing logo will guide the site's colours, visual style and presentation.

What happens after I approve the preview?

We confirm the scope, domain choice, payment and final changes, then arrange the production launch. Preview websites are not official sites until you approve them.
